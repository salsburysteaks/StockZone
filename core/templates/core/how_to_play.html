How to Play

StockZone turns the stock market into a game. No real money. All the competition.

1 The Basics
Start Here

StockZone gives every player $10,000 in paper money to start. You use that balance to make picks, place wagers, and compete with everyone else on the leaderboard — all without risking a single real dollar.

  • Pick stocks to go UP or DOWN each day. If you're right, your win rate climbs. Build a streak and your multiplier grows.
  • Wager your paper balance on your picks. Win and your balance grows. Lose and it shrinks. Keep it above zero.
  • Compete on the leaderboard. Every player is ranked by win rate. The best picker in the game sits at #1.
  • Trade the paper portfolio. Buy and sell real stocks at real prices — no risk, but real market conditions.
Results are resolved at 4:00 PM ET each weekday when US markets close. Picks lock in the moment you submit — you can resubmit and your latest picks replace the previous ones.
2 Daily Picks
Core Feature

Every day the market is open, StockZone shows you the top 5 movers in five sectors — Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Energy, and Consumer. That's 25 stocks to pick from.

What is momentum?

Momentum is how much a stock's price moved today compared to yesterday. A stock up +3% today has strong positive momentum. A stock down -1.5% has negative momentum. We rank each sector's stocks by momentum so you always see the biggest movers first.

Your pick: UP or DOWN?

▲ UP — you think it closes higher today ▼ DOWN — you think it closes lower today

You can pick anywhere from 1 to 25 stocks per day. The more you pick correctly, the higher your multiplier — but your odds per pick depend on each stock's momentum.

The multiplier & wager system:

Each stock has its own odds based on how much it moved today. High momentum stocks are favorites — they pay less because everyone expects them to keep moving in the same direction. Low momentum stocks are underdogs — they pay more because their direction is harder to call.

It works exactly like a sportsbook: the house sets odds based on probability. Pick more underdogs correctly and your multiplier compounds fast. Your total multiplier is the product of every individual stock's odds — capped at 100×.
High momentum (>50%) 1.1× – 1.3× per pick
Mid momentum (20–50%) 1.4× – 1.6× per pick
Low momentum (<20%) 1.7× – 2.2× per pick
3 Parlays
High Risk · High Reward

A parlay chains multiple picks together into a single high-stakes bet. Every leg of the parlay must win for you to collect. Miss even one and the whole parlay loses.

Think of it like a 4-team NFL parlay — one upset kills it. But if you nail all four, your payout multiplies at 1.5× per correct leg. The more legs, the higher the ceiling and the higher the risk.

Each correct leg multiplies your payout by 1.5×:

Legs Formula Multiplier
2 Legs 1.5 × 1.5 2.25×
3 Legs 1.5³ 3.38×
4 Legs 1.5⁴ 5.06×
5 Legs 1.5⁵ 7.59×

Parlays resolve the same time as daily picks — 4:00 PM ET on the pick date. Build your parlay on the Parlay page before the market closes.

4 Future Bets
Long-Term Calls

Instead of calling today's movement, a future bet asks you to predict a stock's direction over a longer timeframe — 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, or a full month.

The longer the timeframe, the harder it is to be right — and the more you get paid if you are. A 1-month call on a volatile stock is a bold prediction, and the 10× payout reflects that.

Payout multipliers by timeframe:

Timeframe Description Payout
3 Days Short-term momentum call 2×
1 Week Weekly direction bet 3×
2 Weeks Mid-term swing trade 5×
1 Month Long-term conviction play 10×

Your wager is locked in when you place the bet. If your direction is right when the bet resolves, wager × multiplier is credited to your paper portfolio balance.

5 Paper Portfolio
Zero Risk · Real Prices

Paper trading means you're investing with fake money at real prices. Every price you see in StockZone is a live market price — but no real money ever changes hands.

  • $10,000 starting balance — enough to build a diversified portfolio from day one.
  • Buy and sell any stock in the StockZone universe at real-time prices.
  • Track your holdings — see unrealized gains, losses, and total portfolio value update live.
  • Learn without losing. Paper trading is how professional traders test strategies before going live. Now it's your training ground.
Your paper balance is also used for daily pick wagers, parlays, and future bets. Everything flows through the same account — picks well and your balance grows, giving you more firepower for bigger plays.
6 Leaderboard
Compete

Every player is ranked by win rate — the percentage of daily picks you've called correctly across all time. The more you play, the more meaningful your ranking becomes.

🥇 #1 Highest win rate
🔥 Streak Consecutive correct picks
📊 Win Rate Your all-time accuracy

There are two leaderboards: the Portfolio Leaderboard ranks players by paper balance, and the Picks Leaderboard ranks by win rate. Dominate both to cement yourself as the top player.

Your win rate only counts resolved picks — PENDING picks don't affect your ranking until 4 PM ET. Check back after market close each day to see how you climbed (or fell).
