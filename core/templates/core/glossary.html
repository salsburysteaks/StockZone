Stock 101

Every term explained like a sports analyst would

Stock
A slice of ownership in a company
Like buying a share of a sports franchise
Why It MattersKnowing what you own helps you understand why a price moves
Market Cap
The total value of a company
Think total franchise valuation
Why It MattersBigger companies are more stable, smaller ones are more volatile and risky
P/E Ratio
How much investors pay per dollar of earnings
Like paying $40 for every $1 of points scored
Why It MattersA very high P/E means investors expect big future growth, or the stock is overpriced
Volume
How many shares traded today
The crowd size at the game
Why It MattersHigh volume confirms a big price move is real, low volume means it might not last
52 Week High
The highest price the stock has hit in the past year
The season record
Why It MattersHitting a new high can signal momentum, or signal the stock is running out of room
Previous Close
What the stock closed at yesterday
Last game's final score
Why It MattersGives you a baseline to judge whether today is actually a big move or just noise
Day %
How much the stock moved today vs yesterday
Today's performance vs last game
Why It MattersThe raw score for today, the number that determines if your pick wins or loses
Volatility
How wildly a stock swings in price
A boom or bust player with huge highs and lows
Why It MattersHigh volatility means bigger potential wins on parlays but also bigger risk of losing
Parlay
Chaining multiple picks together for a multiplied payout
Win them all or walk away empty
Why It MattersOne miss kills the whole chain, high risk but the payout can be massive
Odds
The potential payout on a pick
Higher odds mean higher risk and higher reward
Why It MattersAlways check the odds before locking in, they tell you what the market thinks will happen
Paper Trading
Buying and selling stocks with fake money
Practice squad reps with no consequences
Why It MattersUse it to test strategies before you ever risk real money
Win Rate
Percentage of picks you got right
Your season record as a picker
Why It MattersThe true measure of how good your instincts are over time
